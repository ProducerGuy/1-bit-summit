# Trials register

Every script or run the project intends, with the justification the plan requires: which milestone or idea it moves, what each outcome changes, and why reading cannot answer it. Nothing here has run. Trials are carried out by a lower-cost agent from a written specification, on a local task branch, and nothing merges without the project owner's approval. The ranking of the model runs by probability is in `ranked-work.md`.

## 3. Order

- S1, S2 and S3 are independent of each other and of everything else. S4 needs the coordinate check that opens S2. S4's result is read before the specifications of M2 and M3 are written.
- M1 comes before any training run.
- M2 comes before M3. The stage order is measured on our teacher (idea I4).
- M4 is science scoreboard only and waits on nothing after S2.

## 4. Cost notes

- The published recipe nearest to M2 and M3 used 268 million tokens for its scale stage and 2.1 billion for its reasoning stage at 1.7B. The ladder in M2 starts far below that and reads the slope.
- A teacher can stand in for most of a token budget: in BitNet Distillation, 10 billion tokens of further pre-training add 0.13 points once distillation from the teacher is in place (88.04 to 88.17 on its Qwen3-0.6B task).
- Every trial reports four ledgers kept apart: memory used only in training; total conversion compute, counting the student's generated tokens, the teacher's passes and the optimizer's tokens; operations at inference; stored bits.
- A published reference for training the Qwen3-1.7B body at 1 bit: 20 billion tokens of next-token training with the embedding in full precision keeps 73% of the teacher on eight easy tasks (42.3 of 58.2), and the best accelerator adds 0.1 point (WinQ).
- Published scale training with codes frozen flattens early: accuracy is flat after about two thousand sequences of 4,096 tokens at 2 bits on 7B (EfficientQAT), and a quarter of a million tokens moved a 3B model at 1 bit by 6 points (All for 1-Bit). M2's ladder can be short.
- Published 1-bit training from full precision without a teacher stops improving near 30 billion tokens (ParetoQ). That is the price of a rebuild, which the released model has already paid.
- Every model run needs the ruler accepted first. A score from an unaccepted ruler is not used.
