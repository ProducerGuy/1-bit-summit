# Ideas register

Every idea that could move the ladder or explain the damage and has not been proven or closed. An idea leaves this register in one of two ways: a trial in `trials.md` proves it, or evidence closes it. Probabilities are judgments. "A step" means five points of retention on the ruler (`ruler-spec.md`).

| I1 | Function-preserving group assignment | Second reviewer | Reorder feed-forward neurons per layer and residual channels once for the whole model, both exact symmetries folded into the weights, so that the 128 weights sharing a scale are alike. No stored bits, no runtime step | The algebra is exact. A 4-bit diffusion method gains from sorting channels into scale groups (PermuQuant, arXiv 2605.09503) | The stronger form is measured on two real models: free column scaling, which removes all column-size differences where sorting only groups them, is worth 0.2% to 2.9% of weight error in the body; on the embedding it cut weight error by a quarter and the converted model was no better (`PLAN.md`, sections 2J and 2M). In the feed-forward block the scaling symmetry does everything sorting can at the same zero cost. The idea is new only for the residual stream, where scaling is not a symmetry and reordering is, and there one order must serve every layer and the embedding. JointQ's authors built a search that moves columns between groups (stored as a permutation the loader undoes), list it as a beta feature and leave it off in every reported result; their own test records a 12% drop in a layer objective at 3 bits with regularization on and a negligible one with it off (`research/batch-2-findings.md`, section 2B) | Any measurable gain 20%. A step: under 5% by the first reviewer. By the second: 20% after batch 2; after batch 4, 19% alone and 24% together with scale fitting. Batch 4 adds that two more sources list the same exact symmetries and neither measures one at 2 bits or below, and that a regrouping cannot be applied to a model that is already binary (`research/batch-4-comparison.md`) | S3 |

## 2. Rules the ideas above must respect

- An idea earns nothing until it raises retention on the ruler.
- An idea that changes anything at inference is declared as such and is not counted as a conversion result.
- Weight error does not predict function. A gain shown only in weight space opens a model-level check; it closes nothing.
